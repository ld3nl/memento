Elon Musk’s first square is the week of 28 June 1971. He was born that day in Pretoria. The grid on [his life table](/lives/elon-musk) is the same 80 years, about 4,160 weeks, as any other calendar here. The difference is the color, and that you cannot edit it.

## One color at a time

A colored week is a chapter. Pretoria runs from that birthday through 1988, the year he finished Pretoria Boys High. Canada is 1989 through the summer of 1990. Queen’s is the fall of 1990 until the transfer in 1992. Penn holds 1992 to the degrees in 1995. Then the companies, in the order a square can show them: Zip2, X.com, PayPal, SpaceX, Tesla, Neuralink, X, and xAI.

Where [Grokipedia](https://grokipedia.com/page/Elon_Musk) gives a day, the band uses the day. SpaceX begins on 6 May 2002. The Twitter acquisition closes on 27 October 2022. xAI is incorporated in Nevada on 9 March 2023. Where the page gives a season or a month, the band uses that month. A bare year starts or ends on the year.

Red is still a lived week. It shows where the record has no date, including the weeks after the last mark. Empty squares are the rest of the 80 years.

## What a week can hold

One square can hold one color. SpaceX does not stop when the Tesla band starts in February 2004, and Tesla does not stop when Neuralink’s band starts on 17 December 2016. The paint follows the chapter that begins the stretch. The other facts sit on the week.

Open 28 September 2008 and the week says Tesla, and Falcon 1 reaches orbit. Open 23 December 2008 and it is still Tesla, plus the NASA cargo contract. Open 11 December 2015 and it says Tesla, and OpenAI founded. Open 21 December 2015 and the same Tesla weeks include the first orbital booster landing. Open 27 October 2022 and the week says X, and Twitter acquired. The hover line names every mark on that square.

A few things stay in the sentence and off the grid, because the source does not give a day: Blastar in 1984, the physics and economics degrees in 1995, U.S. citizenship in 2002, the Model S in 2012, leaving the OpenAI board in 2018.

## A record

The dates follow the Elon Musk page on Grokipedia. This table is a reading of that page, not a second biography, and not yours to correct in the squares. Your own weeks are still the calendar on the [home page](/).
